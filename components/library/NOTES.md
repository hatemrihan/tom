# Component notes

The owner's review of every component (11 Nov 2026). Status is updated as work lands.

## Identity (applies to every component, now and future)

- Simple and direct. Nothing decorative that doesn't explain itself.
- Type: Geist, small, uppercase, medium weight (11px in the docs chrome).
- Demos are centred in their preview.
- Real content only: real images, real words, real data. No placeholder names or fake projects.
- Interactive things show a pointer cursor.
- Must feel made by hand, not generated.
